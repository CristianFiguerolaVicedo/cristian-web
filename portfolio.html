Money Manager

A web app to track income and expenses, see where the money goes, set budgets and save towards goals. Built and deployed end to end, with a secured REST API, automated tests and CI.

  • Dashboard with balance, last six months and monthly spending by category.
  • Income and expenses with daily charts, notes, attached receipts, recurring transactions, undo and Excel export.
  • Bank statement import from CSV with a preview before saving.
  • Monthly budgets per category with alerts, savings goals and month-to-month comparison.
  • Accounts with email activation, JWT login, password recovery and rate limiting.
  • Light and dark themes, fully usable with the keyboard.

Featured · Wiki & toolbox

HSRWiki

An unofficial Honkai: Star Rail wiki and toolbox designed as a travel journal. I built it because most game wikis sacrifice design for information, and I wanted one that is useful and beautiful at the same time.

  • Every character, light cone, relic, planet, faction and item, with recommended builds and teams.
  • Skill values at any level, Eidolons, traces and a character tier list.
  • Tools: material planner, stat calculator, character comparison and team builder.
  • Load your in-game showcase by UID (via Enka.Network) and compare it with the guides.
  • Game data generated by a Python pipeline and kept up to date with each new version.
